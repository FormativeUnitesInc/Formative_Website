The problem

Influencer deals run on screenshots, DMs, and hope.

Self-reported follower counts. Terms buried in chat threads. Payment chased over email. Both sides take on risk they can't see — and good partnerships fall apart over it.

  • Brands can't tell which numbers are real — or which creators actually deliver.
  • Creators do the work first and hope the payment shows up.
  • Nothing is binding, so nothing is safe.
The Formative way

Trust, built into every step.

We verify the numbers, verify the humans, hold the money, sign the terms, and let reputation compound. The result is a marketplace where a match means something — from the very first deal.

The trust core

Five guarantees underneath every campaign

Most marketplaces bolt trust on afterward. Formative is built around it.

Verified metrics

Follower and engagement data is pulled straight from authenticated platform APIs — never typed in by hand. The number on the media kit is the real number.

Verified identity

Creators verify who they are with ID.me before a profile goes live. Brands are checked on identity and company legitimacy. Real humans on both sides.

Escrowed money

Campaign funds are held safely and released only for work the brand approves. Creators know the money is there before they start.

Signed contracts

Agreed terms become a binding, e-signed contract — not a promise lost in a chat thread. Both sides are protected on paper.

Earned reputation

Ratings and real campaign outcomes build a track record that can't be faked — and a strong record compounds into better deals and higher rates.

Trust that can't be copied

Verified metrics, daily snapshots, and real outcomes accumulate into a record a new competitor literally can't buy or backfill.
