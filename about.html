About Formative

We're building trust into the creator economy

The creator economy runs on relationships — but the deals behind them run on screenshots, DMs, and hope. Formative exists to change that, for both sides.

Our mission

Make every creator partnership safe, transparent, and fair

Brands should know the numbers are real and their spend is protected. Creators should know the work is legitimate and the money will arrive. Neither should have to take the other on faith.

So we built the marketplace around trust instead of bolting it on afterward — verifying the metrics, verifying the humans, holding the money, signing the terms, and letting reputation compound from the very first deal.

What we believe

The principles behind the product

Proof over promises

If a number can be verified, we verify it. Discovery and matching run only on authenticated data — never on what someone typed into a box.

Both sides, protected

A marketplace only works when it's fair. Every safeguard we build protects the creator and the brand at the same time.

Plain-language honesty

No dark patterns, no inflated claims. We explain how things work in plain terms — including the parts that are still a work in progress.

Why now

The creator economy grew up. Its infrastructure didn't.

Billions flow through sponsored content every year, yet the deals still hinge on trust that nobody can verify. Formative brings the verification, contracts, and payment protection that every other serious market already takes for granted — and makes them native to how creators and brands actually work.
