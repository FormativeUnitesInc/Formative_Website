The opportunity

The school check isn't the whole game.

Since 2025, schools can pay athletes directly — but that money is concentrated on a handful of players in football and men's basketball. For everyone else, the real, lasting lever is the audience and brand you build yourself.

  • Your audience is the asset you keep — long after eligibility ends.
  • A clear brand opens third-party deals in every division and most states.
  • Start now and you walk into the next level already ahead.

Built for compliance

Play it safe. Know your lane.

NIL rules vary by division, state, and citizenship status — and they change. Our materials teach the guardrails and point athletes to the right authority. This is education, not legal advice.

Real deals only

Real work — post, appear, endorse. No pay-for-play, no no-show deals, no school logos or booster collectives.

Disclose every deal

College deals over $600 get a fair-value check; high schoolers disclose to their AD with a parent. When in doubt, ask first.

Protect eligibility

A bad deal can cost a season now and eligibility later — and for international (F-1) athletes, a visa. Check first, sign second.
