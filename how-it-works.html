How it works

Trust built into every step.

Both sides onboard in minutes and meet in a single protected deal — from the first match to the final payout. Here's exactly how each piece works.

The deal flow

From brief to payout, in six moves

The edges of both journeys were already familiar. Formative's difference is the middle — where trust and money live.

01 · Verify

Real humans, real numbers

Creators connect authenticated socials and verify identity with ID.me. Brands verify identity and company legitimacy. Only verified profiles become discoverable.

02 · Match

Ranked by affinity

The engine scores and ranks creators against the brand's brief and priorities, then an AI layer curates and explains the shortlist.

03 · Agree

Structured offers

Both sides compare verified media kits and negotiate rate and deliverables with structured offers — not a lossy chat thread.

04 · Sign

A binding contract

Agreed terms generate a contract both parties e-sign. The deal now has a real, enforceable artifact.

05 · Fund escrow

Money held safely

The brand funds the campaign into escrow before any work starts. Both sides see the funded state.

06 · Deliver & pay

Approve → auto-payout

The creator delivers, the brand approves, and the escrowed milestone releases automatically. Outcomes build reputation.

The matching engine, in plain terms

How the match score really works

Add up how well a creator fits on each thing that matters, weighted by what this brand cares about. That's the whole idea — the rest is rigor.

Seven signals, one 0–100 score

  • Audience alignment — age, gender, and location to city level.
  • Niche & content fit — derived from real posts and style.
  • Engagement & reach — followers, views, and real reactions.
  • Track record — past campaigns, ratings, delivery.
  • Price fit — their rate versus your budget.
  • Brand safety — content rating, verification, risk flags.
  • Competitive history — recent competitor work.

Match tiers

Best Match85–100
Strong Match70–84
Possible Match50–69

The engine works with incomplete data — a nano-creator with 400 followers is scored on the information available, never penalized for gaps.

Personalized before your first search

When a brand joins, Formative shows realistic but fictional creator profiles and learns from quick comparisons. In a couple of minutes we learn what a brand actually values — so the engine is personalized from the very first search, with no cold-start.

AI explains — it doesn't override

After ranking, an AI layer narrows the top candidates and adds human-sounding reasons like "the chef's credibility matches your wood-fired story." This layer only explains and curates. It never secretly changes the underlying score, which stays fully auditable.

The trust core

Five guarantees, one by one

Trust isn't a badge on Formative — it's the architecture.

1

Verified metrics

The numbers on every media kit are verified by the platforms themselves, not self-reported. Matching, discovery, and visibility are computed only from authenticated first-party data.

2

Verified identity

Creators verify identity once with ID.me before becoming discoverable. Brands are verified on two independent axes — individual identity and company legitimacy — decoupled from payment-method checks to stop impersonation.

3

Escrowed money

Campaign funds are held safely and released only for approved work — with partial-release logic when performance targets are partly met. If a creator delivers, they get paid. Period.

4

Signed contracts

Agreed terms become a binding, e-signed contract instead of promises buried in chat. Both sides are explicitly protected.

5

Earned reputation

Authentic ratings and real outcomes compound into a track record that can't be faked — and that record gets richer the longer a creator is on Formative.

A moat that can't be backfilled

Because platforms don't allow historical backfilling, the verified metrics, daily snapshots, and real campaign outcomes Formative accumulates become uniquely defensible. The longer the marketplace runs, the richer and more unique that record becomes — something a new competitor literally cannot buy.
